Gruha Lakshmi (Gruha Lakshmi Scheme) - Government of Karnataka

Scheme name: Gruha Lakshmi / Gruhalakshmi
Level: State (Karnataka)
Department: Women and Child Development / Government of Karnataka guarantee schemes
Category: Women welfare / income support

Monthly assistance amount:
- Eligible woman head of the family receives Rs 2,000 (two thousand rupees) per month
- Assistance is transferred to the beneficiary bank account (DBT)

Who is eligible (summary):
- Woman must be a resident of Karnataka
- Woman should be the head of the family (or recognized as such under scheme rules)
- Family should belong to the eligible category as notified by the Government of Karnataka
- Applicant should have a valid Aadhaar-linked bank account for Direct Benefit Transfer
- Only one woman per family is generally eligible under the scheme norms

Documents commonly required:
- Aadhaar
- Bank account details / passbook
- Address / residence proof in Karnataka
- Family / ration card details as required on Seva Sindhu / department portal

How to apply:
- Applications are commonly submitted through Seva Sindhu (sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in) and related Karnataka e-governance channels
