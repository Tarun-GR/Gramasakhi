Anna Bhagya Scheme - Government of Karnataka

Scheme name: Anna Bhagya
Level: State (Karnataka)
Department: Food, Civil Supplies and Consumer Affairs, Government of Karnataka
Category: Food security / public distribution

Benefits (summary):
- Provides subsidized / free food grains to eligible ration card holders in Karnataka as per current government notifications
- Quantity and price are set by Karnataka Food and Civil Supplies notifications and may be revised

Eligibility (summary):
- Must hold a valid Karnataka ration card under the Public Distribution System
- Priority / Antyodaya / eligible household categories as notified by the state
