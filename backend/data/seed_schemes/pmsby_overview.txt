Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY) - Scheme Overview (Central Government)

Scheme name: Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY)
Ministry / Department: Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance, Government of India
Category: Accident insurance / social security

Benefits / cover amount:
- Accidental death: Rs 2 lakh (two lakh rupees) sum insured
- Permanent total disability due to accident: Rs 2 lakh
- Permanent partial disability due to accident: Rs 1 lakh (as per scheme schedule)

Premium:
- Annual premium is Rs 20 per member per year (subject to official notifications)
- Premium is typically auto-debited from the linked bank account

Eligibility (summary):
- Indian citizens in the eligible age band as notified (commonly 18 to 70 years)
- Must have a savings bank account and give consent for auto-debit
- One cover per eligible member through a participating bank

Enrolment / claim:
- Enrolment is through participating banks
- Claims require bank facilitation and prescribed documents such as claim form, KYC, and accident/death proof as applicable

Source note: Official Central Government social security scheme (PMSBY) under the Jan Suraksha umbrella administered with Department of Financial Services.
