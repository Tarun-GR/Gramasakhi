Karnataka Shakti Scheme (Free Bus Travel for Women)

Scheme name: Shakti Scheme (also referred to as Shakti Yojana)
State: Karnataka, India
Department: Transport Department, Government of Karnataka
Official citizen portals: https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in/ and https://www.myscheme.gov.in/

Purpose:
The Shakti scheme provides free travel for eligible women passengers on participating
state-run bus services in Karnataka. It is a state government welfare scheme intended
to improve women's mobility and access to education, work, and essential services.

Eligibility (public scheme description):
- Women citizens of Karnataka are the intended beneficiaries for free travel under the scheme.
- Travel is available on participating Karnataka State Road Transport Corporation (KSRTC)
  and other notified state transport buses as per government notifications.
- Beneficiaries should follow the latest official rules for proof of identity when required
  by conductors or transport authorities.
- Men are not eligible for free travel under this scheme.
- Rules for children, special categories, and exclusions are as notified by the Transport
  Department from time to time.

Benefits:
- Zero fare for eligible women passengers on notified routes and participating bus services.
- Applies to ordinary / notified categories of state buses as per the government order;
  premium or excluded services (if any) follow the latest official notification.

How to use / apply:
- Eligible women may board participating buses and travel without paying the ordinary fare,
  subject to verification rules published by the Transport Department.
- For grievance redressal, route information, or scheme clarifications, citizens should use
  Seva Sindhu or the Transport Department's official channels.
- Always verify the latest eligibility and bus categories on official government portals
  before travel, because notifications may be updated.

Documents commonly referenced:
- Government-issued photo identity proof may be requested (for example Aadhaar or other
  accepted ID), as per current conductor / transport guidelines.
- No separate online application is required merely to board for free travel when the
  scheme is active and the passenger is eligible, unless a future notification says otherwise.

Important:
- This note summarizes publicly described scheme information for citizen assistance.
- Exact fare exemptions, bus categories, and identity checks follow the latest Government
  of Karnataka Transport Department notification.
- Official sources: Seva Sindhu (https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in/), myScheme
  (https://www.myscheme.gov.in/), and Karnataka Transport Department publications.
