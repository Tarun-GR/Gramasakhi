Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

Scheme name: Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)
Scope: Central Sector / Centrally Sponsored crop insurance scheme
Ministry: Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India
Official portals: https://pmfby.gov.in/ and https://www.myscheme.gov.in/

Purpose:
PMFBY provides crop insurance cover to farmers against yield losses due to non-preventable
natural risks such as drought, flood, pest attack, and other notified perils, as defined
in the scheme guidelines for the insured season and crop.

Eligibility (public scheme description):
- All farmers growing notified crops in notified areas can enroll, including loanee and
  non-loanee farmers, subject to state / season notifications.
- Enrollment is typically done through banks, Common Service Centres (CSCs), insurance
  company channels, or the PMFBY portal as notified for the season.
- Coverage applies only for crops and areas notified for that season by the state government.

Benefits:
- Financial compensation for yield loss assessed under the scheme methodology for the
  insured crop and area.
- Premium rates for farmers are subsidized as per the scheme design; the balance premium
  is shared by Central and State Governments according to the prevailing guidelines.
- Exact sum insured, premium, and cut-off dates are season-specific and published in
  official notifications.

Documents / information commonly required at enrollment:
- Land records / sowing certificate as applicable
- Bank account details and Aadhaar as required by the enrollment channel
- Crop and survey / village details for the notified area

Important:
- Farmers should confirm the current season's notified crops, cut-off dates, and premium
  on https://pmfby.gov.in/ or the state agriculture portal before enrollment.
- This summary is for citizen guidance from publicly described scheme information and
  does not replace the official operational guidelines.
